"Reality is a machine. Machines can be repaired."

Doom's defining trait in the comics is not malice, it is engineering. He treats a collapsing multiverse the way an engineer treats a failing bridge: identify the load, replace the structure, book the casualties as a line item.

That is what makes him worse than Thanos. Thanos wanted balance and was willing to kill for it. Doom wants a solution that works, and he has usually already built it before anyone finishes arguing.

"Science asks permission. Sorcery does not."

What separates Doom from every other genius in Marvel is that he refuses to pick a lane. A peer of Reed Richards in the lab and a peer of Strange in the mystic arts, and he considers the distinction a failure of imagination in everyone else.

In the 2019 run reportedly informing Downey's take, he is tormented by visions of a perfect world he cannot reach. A man who can see the correct reality and cannot get to it will eventually try to build it out of the wrong ones.

"A throne is only a chair nobody argues with."

Latveria matters. Doom is a head of state, so his crimes are foreign policy and his experiments are national projects. He does not skulk. He legislates.

In Hickman's Secret Wars he does not conquer Battleworld so much as administrate it. Gods, barons, a legal code, a church. The horror of God-Emperor Doom is not that he rules badly. It is that it functions.

Sentry & the Void

Lewis Pullman returns as Bob Reynolds. The power of a million exploding suns, and a shadow that comes with it. Click the figure to flip him.

The Sentry
The Void
"The power of a million exploding suns."

Robert Reynolds is, on paper, the strongest thing in the room — a Superman-scale hero whose strength has been described as rivalling Thor or the Hulk. In Thunderbolts* he was the emotional centre rather than the muscle: an addict with a split mind who could not be trusted with what he could do.

He ends that film having beaten the darkness with help, and joins the New Avengers. The post-credits scene, set fourteen months later and directed by the Russos themselves, has him admitting he will not risk becoming the Sentry again.

"His biggest Achilles heel is himself."

The Void is not a separate villain. It is the same man. Every time Bob reaches for the power, the shadow comes with it, and in Thunderbolts* it was capable of swallowing a city.

Pullman has said his teammates will not let him out of their sight in Doomsday, because they know what happens if he is caught in the wrong hands or left unsupported. In a film about a man collecting realities, an unstable nuclear option that walks and talks is not a hero. It is an asset someone will try to acquire.

Avengers: Doomsday — release date, cast and everything confirmed

The plain-text version of this entire file. Everything Marvel has actually confirmed about Avengers: Doomsday, updated as news lands.

When does Avengers: Doomsday release?

Avengers: Doomsday releases in theatres worldwide on 18 December 2026. It was originally scheduled for 1 May 2026, and Disney moved it to the December slot in May 2025 — pushing its sequel, Avengers: Secret Wars, to 17 December 2027 at the same time. Principal photography wrapped in September 2025 at Pinewood Studios in the UK, with additional photography carried out afterwards.

Is there an Avengers: Doomsday trailer?

Yes — two of them. The first full trailer debuted to exhibitors at CinemaCon in April 2026 and was released publicly on 20 July 2026, timed to tickets going on sale. It shows the Avengers, the Fantastic Four and the X-Men crossing paths, and delivers its headline reveal: Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom.

A second, longer trailer was then shown only to the Hall H audience at San Diego Comic-Con 2026, alongside a new Doom poster. That version opens on Victor seated on a throne while Sue Storm narrates his backstory — “Victor was always the smartest guy in the room” — establishing that the MCU’s Fantastic Four already know him and share a history with him. Reporting on the footage also places Doom at the head of an army of Sentinels, and gives new looks at Cyclops, Magneto and Steve Rogers.

Is Wolverine in Avengers: Doomsday?

Hugh Jackman is not on the announced cast list, so Wolverine is unconfirmed. Logan and Wolverine are the same character — Logan is the name he goes by, Wolverine the codename — and the case for his return rests on Deadpool & Wolverine, which folded the Fox library into the MCU and ended with this version of him choosing to stay. Treat it as speculation until Marvel says otherwise.

The confirmed mutants are Professor X, Magneto, Cyclops, Mystique, Nightcrawler, Beast and Gambit.

What is Avengers: Doomsday about?

Marvel’s official synopsis is deliberately short: heroes from three distinct universes are set on a deadly collision course and face an existential threat unlike anything they have encountered. The three universes are generally read as the main MCU, the Fantastic Four’s reality from First Steps, and the Fox X-Men timeline.

The mechanic underneath that is the comic-book idea of an incursion: two realities drift close enough together that one must be destroyed for the other to survive. It is not a villain, it is physics with a deadline — and it is the engine of Jonathan Hickman’s New Avengers and Secret Wars runs, which Marvel has pointed to as source material. Doom’s answer in those books is to stop trying to save the branches separately and instead weld the survivors into one patchwork world under a single ruler.

Frequently asked questions

Is Avengers: Doomsday the same as Avengers 5?

Yes. Doomsday is the fifth Avengers film. It was originally announced in 2022 as Avengers: The Kang Dynasty and retitled at San Diego Comic-Con in July 2024, after Marvel parted ways with Jonathan Majors and moved the saga’s focus from Kang to Doctor Doom.

Why is Robert Downey Jr. playing Doctor Doom?

Marvel has not explained it in-story. The dominant fan theory is that Doom is a multiversal variant connected to Tony Stark, which would make the resemblance a plot point rather than a casting quirk. Nothing about that has been confirmed — treat it as speculation until December.

Is Iron Man coming back?

Not as Tony Stark. Downey returns in the same December tentpole playing a different character entirely. Tony died at the end of Endgame and Marvel has not undone that.

Is Deadpool in Avengers: Doomsday?

Ryan Reynolds is not on the announced cast list, though his appearance at Comic-Con 2026 kept the rumours alive. Unconfirmed either way.

Do I need to watch the old X-Men films first?

Helpful, not required. X2 and Days of Future Past are the most useful for reacquainting yourself with Xavier, Magneto and Mystique, since these are the same actors rather than recasts.

How long is Avengers: Doomsday?

Marvel has not published a runtime. Expect it closer to release.

Will Avengers: Doomsday stream on Disney+?

It opens exclusively in theatres on 18 December 2026. Marvel titles typically reach Disney+ a few months after their theatrical run, but no streaming date has been announced.
