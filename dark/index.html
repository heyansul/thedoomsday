EXHIBIT 01 · THE PLANET

Earth, afterwards

Held together by nothing but momentum

An incursion does not explode a world. It presses two of them together until one gives, and what gives goes quietly — crust first, then oceans, then everything that was standing on top of it. The pieces stay in orbit around each other for a while out of habit.

The ash is the part nobody writes about. Everything that burned is still up here, falling slowly through a gravity that cannot decide where its centre is. It has been falling for a long time. It will keep falling.

"Reality is a machine. Machines can be repaired." — and this is the machine, mid-repair, with the housing off.
EXHIBIT 02 · THE LEDGER

What it already cost

Before Doom. Before any of this was his

This part is not a theory. These three were paid out in full, on screen, and the universe has not given any of them back.

Tony Stark
ENDGAME · 2019

Took six stones into a gauntlet built for a titan, snapped with a human hand, and did not survive the radiation. The fan reading says he was this timeline’s anchor being — that the crack everything is falling through is the shape of him leaving.

Natasha Romanoff
VORMIR · A SOUL FOR A SOUL

Went to a cliff with the only person who would have taken her place and made sure she got there first. There is no undo on the Soul Stone. Everyone came back except the person who bought them.

Steve Rogers
RETURNED THE STONES, STAYED

Not killed — kept. He went back to put the stones where they belonged and chose not to come home the long way round. The man who sat down on that bench was ninety-odd years old and done. Evans returns in December; that is not the same as him being back.

Add the ones the ledger usually skips: Vision, killed twice in ten minutes by the two people who loved him most. Gamora, thrown off the same cliff as Natasha by a father who called it love. Wanda, who brought a mountain down on herself once she understood what she had been doing.

EXHIBIT 03 · THE ERASURE

Peter and MJ

The quietest catastrophe in the whole saga

It was not Doom and it was not an incursion. It was a spell, cast on purpose, by a friend, at Peter’s own request. Everyone in the world forgot Peter Parker existed. Not Spider-Man — Peter. The name, the face, the boy.

He goes to the coffee shop where she works with the cut still on her forehead from the fight she does not remember. He gets as far as her taking his order. Then he decides that a life she does not remember is safer than a life she does, and he orders the coffee, and he leaves.

She asks him what his name is. He tells her. It does not mean anything to her, and that is the whole point of it working.

What is left is a young man with a hand-sewn suit in a bare apartment, no Stark money, no May, no Ned, no MJ, and nobody on Earth who could confirm he was ever anybody’s son. In a story about realities being deleted, Peter is the one character who has already been deleted from one and had to keep going anyway.

Then Brand New Day ends with him leaving the planet entirely. Read that however you like.

EXHIBIT 04 · THE OTHERS

Everyone else, filed

Dark readings, clearly labelled as readings
THE X-MEN

A timeline that ran for twenty-six years and then found out it was a branch. If the comics hold, one of the two Earths in an incursion does not survive it — and Marvel did not bring six original actors back to have them stand at the edge of the frame. The darkest version of this film is the one where saving 616 means agreeing to lose them.

THE SENTRY

A man with the power of a million exploding suns who will not use it, watched constantly by teammates who know why. In the comics, Reed Richards and Doctor Strange erased him from the world’s memory to keep the Void buried. Both of those men are in this film. That precedent is sitting there like a loaded thing on a table.

FRANKLIN RICHARDS

Powerful enough that Galactus wanted him as a successor. Doom needs a Beyonder-scale source to build Battleworld. Somewhere in the dark version of this story, two parents are asked to choose between one child and every reality there is, and the film does not let them off.

LOKI

Sitting at the end of time holding every branch in his hands, alone, permanently, so that the rest of it can keep happening. He is not ruling the multiverse. He is load-bearing for it. Nobody is coming to relieve him.

EXHIBIT 05 · THE ARGUMENT

And then Doom

The part that is genuinely hard to answer

He looks at all of the above — the ash, the ledger, the girl who does not know her boyfriend’s name — and he says: this happened while you were in charge. Not to hurt you. As data.

Then he offers to weld the survivors into one world with one law and one ruler, and books the rest as a line item. It is monstrous. It also, in the comics, works, and keeps working for years, and the people living inside it are alive in a way they were not on the way there.

Refusing him is a principle. It is not a plan. Nobody in this file has a plan.

That is Doom’s dilemma, and it is why the loop is so hard to leave: every road out of it that he modelled leads back to him being right. The only exit is a branch he never accounted for — and the only way to find it is to take everything else off the tree first.

Go and break it
